GTA6 Factory

Personal tool — not a public service.

GTA6 Factory is a private, personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to prepare short videos about the upcoming game Grand Theft Auto VI and to upload them, after manual review, to the owner's own YouTube channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API only on channels belonging to its owner: to upload videos (as private or scheduled), to read basic channel information, and to read view statistics of the owner's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

Fan project. Not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Google.
