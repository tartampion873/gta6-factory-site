Privacy policy

GTA6 Factory · last updated 5 November 2026

GTA6 Factory is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own YouTube channels. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

Google user data accessed

  • youtube.upload — to upload the owner's own videos, as private or scheduled.
  • youtube.readonly — to confirm which channel is connected and read the status of the uploaded videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read view and retention statistics of the owner's own videos, used to decide which video formats work best.

How the data is stored and shared

Authorization tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of the YouTube API is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data.
